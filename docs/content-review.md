# Content feasibility review

Reviewed: 2026-09-13. Scope: ten ingredients, Sweden, monthly domestic availability. This is a local evidence sample, not a completed launch decision.

**Historical baseline:** The source comparison below describes the original ICA-only sample. The [source decision and gap review](source-decision.md) records the current policy and applied corrections for raspberries, carrots and plums.

## Source choice and interpretation

The sample uses [ICA's Swedish seasonal calendar](https://www.ica.se/artikel/svenska-gronsaker-och-frukt-i-sasong/) as a single provisional annual reference. Its twelve lists can be inspected directly. The introduction explicitly includes stored domestic crops. It is a retailer's consumer guide, not a harvest survey or real-time stock feed. No publication/update date was established; the checked date is not its publication date.

[Från Sverige](https://fransverige.se/om-fran-sverige/sasongsguider/) remains a candidate for the final reference. Three posters were visually inspected for comparison. This limited comparison is not independent validation of every month and not proof that either source is exhaustive. ICA also participates in the industry represented in Svenskmärkning; the sources should not be described as wholly independent.

The source's month headings, together with each ingredient's `source_terms`, locate the evidence. All twelve ICA lists were read. A positive listing supports an availability claim. An omission is recorded as unknown. No season boundaries, midpoint peaks, regional offsets or storage months are inferred.

## Spot comparison

The Från Sverige landing page currently links January–April 2027 and May–December 2026 posters. Preserve this edition distinction: these are not twelve observed harvest reports from one year.

- [January 2027](https://fransverige.se/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Sasongsguide_2027_01.pdf): the four sample ingredients listed by ICA in January also appear on the poster.
- [June 2026](https://fransverige.se/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Sasongsguide_2026_06.pdf): corroborates several summer entries, but also includes raspberries. ICA omits raspberries from June. Conversely, the poster omits some ICA entries. These lists must not be silently merged into a supposedly complete calendar.
- [September 2026](https://fransverige.se/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Sasongsguide_2026_09.pdf): several sample entries agree; some are absent from the poster. Absence alone is not a contradictory claim.

The PDFs inspected are image-based. Text extraction alone returned no text; visual review was necessary. Any future OCR output needs checking against the page image.

## What this establishes

- It is possible to build a useful, source-attributed monthly sample without guessing missing values.
- Stored produce makes winter useful, but the evidence does not support labelling exact storage versus harvest periods throughout the year.
- A single source provides a tractable initial structure, but its omissions leave visible gaps. A fuller launch catalogue needs editorial decisions about those gaps and the preferred reference.
- Illustrations exist for all ten sample ingredients, under an explicit CC0 licence. The pear image is a generic reference illustration for this interface; it does not depict every cultivar named by the calendar. The asparagus image depicts the green form.
